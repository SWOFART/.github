# SWOFART

Welcome to **SWOFART** — an open-source organization and community building useful, creative, and meaningful projects together.

We believe great software is built in the open: through collaboration, shared knowledge, and contributions from people with different backgrounds and experiences.

## What we do

We create and maintain open-source projects that help people learn, build, and solve real-world problems. Our work may include:

- Developer tools and libraries
- Community-focused applications
- Experiments and innovative ideas
- Educational resources
- Projects built collaboratively by our members

## Our latest projects

- [open-circuit](https://github.com/SWOFART/open-circuit) — A TypeScript project exploring open and creative software development.
- [OneShot](https://github.com/SWOFART/OneShot) — Protects approved business obligations from duplicate committed settlements across retries, crashes, lost responses, queue redelivery, parallel workers, and multiple agent instances.

## Get involved

Everyone is welcome to participate, whether you are an experienced developer or just getting started. You can contribute by:

- Exploring our repositories
- Reporting bugs or suggesting improvements
- Fixing issues and submitting pull requests
- Improving documentation
- Sharing feedback and ideas
- Helping other community members

Before contributing, please check the contribution guidelines and code of conduct in the repository you are interested in.

## Our community

SWOFART is built by its community. We value:

- Open and respectful collaboration
- Clear communication
- Learning and sharing knowledge
- Inclusive participation
- Practical, maintainable solutions

## Explore our projects

Browse our repositories to discover what we are building. If something interests you, feel free to try it, provide feedback, or join the discussion.

## Stay connected

Follow the organization to keep up with new projects, releases, and community updates. Contributions of all kinds are appreciated — every improvement helps.
